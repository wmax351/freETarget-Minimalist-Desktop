Shot Quality & Grouping
How to Read These Statistics

Score % = points earned ÷ (recorded shots × the saved scoring maximum): 10 for integer, 10.9 for decimal. Partial sessions use only their recorded shots. Pistol and rifle, and integer and decimal scoring, stay separate. Filters apply to the chart and session list.

The overview average is weighted by recorded shots. Best and latest are individual session percentages. Change compares the latest with the earliest session in the filtered period; it is a descriptive change, not a prediction. The dashed chart line is the trailing average of up to 10 visible sessions. Different formats and shot counts can still affect comparisons.

Mean radius measures hit distances from each session’s own group center; lower means a tighter group. Group spread is the greatest center-to-center distance between two hits. Center offset is the distance from the group’s average impact to target center. These values use saved target coordinates, without the display circle’s visual padding. The Mean Impact X/Y card pools the selected hits; opposite session offsets can cancel. Group size depends on shot count; use similar formats for comparisons.

Shot score SD measures score variation, not aiming stability. 10-ring, 9+ and X rates use recorded shots with available data. Zeros and penalties count in scoring; misses and synthetic penalty positions are excluded from grouping. Sighting shots are excluded. Sessions with ignored misses and partial sessions are included unless explicitly excluded. Demo data is separate from real shooting history. Missing data is shown as unavailable; no values are invented. Aim trace, hold stability and trigger timing are not recorded by this app.

Sources: TargetScan: Score, Mean Radius And Impact Position · Standard Deviation Definition

Shooters, guns, History, settings and the custom logo are saved in this app’s local database. App updates use the same data folder.
